About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a weekend bread stand created to make handmade food easier to share. Support from neighbors helped the bakery grow into a warm gathering place for everyday breakfasts, family traditions, and local events.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We choose regional flour, dairy, fruit, and honey when they are available. Seasonal menus help us work with fresh ingredients, reduce unnecessary waste, and support nearby producers.
